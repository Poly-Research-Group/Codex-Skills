# Control Variable Selection Audit

For each candidate control, answer:

1. What construct does the variable represent?
2. Why could this construct confound the relationship between the focal explanatory variable and outcome?
3. Is the variable measured before the focal treatment, event, or key independent variable?
4. Could the variable be affected by the treatment or focal independent variable?
5. Could the variable be a mediator or mechanism?
6. Could the variable be a collider?
7. Is the variable mechanically constructed from the dependent variable?
8. Would the variable be absorbed by unit, category, time, or other fixed effects?
9. Should the variable enter the main specification, a robustness specification, a mechanism analysis, or no model?
10. What is the preferred operationalization: baseline, lagged, pre-period mean, time-invariant, or contemporaneous?

Do not include controls based only on statistical significance, fit improvement, or availability.
