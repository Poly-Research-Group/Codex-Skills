# Design Audit Intake Questionnaire

Before running the empirical design audit, complete or answer the following.

1. What is the empirical setting and focal phenomenon?
2. What are the research question and hypotheses?
3. What is the intended unit of analysis and row key?
4. What is the time structure and observation window?
5. What are the dependent variables and when are they measured?
6. What are the key independent variables, treatments, events, or exposures and when are they measured?
7. What are the moderators, mediators, and mechanisms?
8. What candidate controls are proposed, and why should each be included?
9. What sample restrictions were applied and at what stage?
10. What identification strategy is intended?
11. What fixed effects and clustering dimensions are intended?
12. What analysis-ready dataset and metadata files should the audit use?
