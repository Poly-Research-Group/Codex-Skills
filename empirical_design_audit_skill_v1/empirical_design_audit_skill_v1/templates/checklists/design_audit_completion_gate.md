# Design Audit Completion Gate

Before handing off to model selection, confirm:

- [ ] Research question and hypotheses are mapped to empirical variables.
- [ ] Unit of analysis and row key are verified.
- [ ] Time structure is verified.
- [ ] Outcome timing follows the cause/treatment/key IV.
- [ ] Treatment/event/exposure construction is audited.
- [ ] Controls are classified and bad controls are flagged.
- [ ] Sample restrictions and attrition are documented.
- [ ] Fixed effects and clustering dimensions are feasible or risks are documented.
- [ ] Identification-threat register is complete.
- [ ] Estimator-readiness matrix is complete.
- [ ] Go/no-go decision is explicit.
- [ ] Model-selection handoff memo is complete.
